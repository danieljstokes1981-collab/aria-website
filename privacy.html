Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Short version: this website does not have a signup form, does not set cookies of its own, and does not collect anything you type into the live hash demonstration -- it runs entirely in your own browser.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to ariaengaged.co.uk only -- the marketing website you are reading now. It does not cover ARIA the software product, which is licensed to and operated by separate organisations under their own terms, nor any product built on top of ARIA (see the Powered by ARIA page for products still in development).

Data controller

ARIA EngagEd Ltd is the data controller for this website. Contact: daniel.stokes@ariaengaged.co.uk.

What this site does not do

  • No account creation, login, or signup form
  • No cookies set by this site itself
  • No analytics or tracking scripts of any kind
  • No data sent anywhere when you use the live hash demonstration -- it uses your browser's own built-in cryptography (the Web Crypto API) and never transmits what you type

What does happen when you visit

Two things happen that are outside this site's direct control, and are worth being upfront about:

  • Fonts: this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's servers means your browser makes a request to Google, which can see the requesting IP address, in the same way any request to any server on the internet works. Google's own privacy policy governs that request, not this one.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted via GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub's infrastructure logs basic technical request data (such as IP address and request time) for security and operational purposes, governed by GitHub's own privacy policy, not this one.

Contacting us

The contact link on this site opens your own email client via a standard mailto: link. Nothing is submitted to or stored by this website when you click it -- the resulting email goes directly to the address shown, the same as any email you'd send from your own inbox.

Your rights

Because this site does not collect or store personal data of its own, most data-subject rights under UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, and so on) have little to act on here. If you believe that has changed, or you have a concern about this site specifically, contact the address above.

Changes to this policy

If what this site does changes -- for example, if a contact form or analytics is added in future -- this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
