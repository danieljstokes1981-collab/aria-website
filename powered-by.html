EARLY DEVELOPMENT

3Venture

Enterprise control plane & visual dashboard

3Venture operates as the primary enterprise control plane and visual dashboard substrate for the ARIA ecosystem. It ingests real-time cryptographic audit logs and telemetry feeds to provide centralized multi-layer governance oversight. The platform enables non-technical administrators to monitor deterministic agent state compliance across active operational environments.

EARLY DEVELOPMENT

NABU

Early signal pattern recognition tool

NABU operates as a deterministic early signal pattern recognition tool built directly on the ARIA substrate. It ingests complex multi-source data streams to detect pre-incident anomaly signatures and regulatory divergence before threshold breach occurs. By mapping early behavioral telemetry against verified policy invariants, NABU delivers real-time risk intelligence without speculative model drift.

EARLY DEVELOPMENT

Qer

Continuous quality, risk, and evidence verification

Qer functions as a continuous quality, risk, and evidence verification engine that monitors operational agent outputs. It evaluates state transitions against predefined behavioral bounds to construct tamper-evident cryptographic proofs. System operators rely on Qer to deliver real-time auditability and deterministic verification across all critical data workflows.
