Substrate capabilities

Shipped verticals

ARIA ships with working taxonomy for these domains — configured signal definitions, regulatory mappings, and escalation tiers. Each vertical is a tested starting point, not a finished product. Deployment into a regulated context requires a scoping and calibration step with the operator.

Shipped

Safeguarding & Child Protection

Behavioural signal monitoring for regulated environments with child protection obligations. Three escalation tiers: pastoral review, DSL escalation, and statutory reporting pathway.

Children Act 1989 Working Together 2023 KCSIE
Shipped

Education & Student Wellbeing

Monitoring for schools, further education, and higher education supporting early intervention, pastoral care, and student wellbeing obligations.

Education Act OfSTED
Shipped

Financial Conduct & Consumer Duty

Monitoring for FCA-regulated firms under Consumer Duty and SM&CR. Surfaces vulnerability indicators, suitability gaps, and treating-customers-fairly signals.

FCA PS22/9 Consumer Duty SM&CR
Shipped

Financial Crime & Market Integrity

SAR/AML/MAR monitoring with MLRO routing and tipping-off enforcement. Triggers for PEP flags, sanctions proximity, and market abuse indicators at MAR Article 16 thresholds.

POCA 2002 MAR Art.16 SMCR
Shipped

Healthcare & Clinical Wellbeing

Clinical governance and patient safety monitoring for NHS trusts, mental health services, GP surgeries, and care providers.

CQC NHS framework Patient safety
Shipped

Online Platform Safety

Trust and safety monitoring for platforms subject to the Online Safety Act 2023. Supports content moderation triage and user safety obligations for Category 1 and 2A services.

Online Safety Act 2023 Ofcom
Shipped

Sport & Performance Wellbeing

Athlete welfare monitoring for elite sport, performance academies, and sporting organisations under UK Sport and Sport England duty of care frameworks.

UK Sport duty of care Sport England
Shipped

Workplace Wellbeing & Occupational Health

Employee wellbeing monitoring supporting HR referral pathways and occupational health obligations under health and safety and equality legislation.

HSWA 1974 Equality Act 2010
Demonstration

Transmission Ecology

Anomaly detection on experimental virus transmission data. Calibrated against the Wallace & Obbard (2024) empirical dataset — 606 individuals, 24 viruses, 3 Drosophila species. Demonstrates that ARIA processes real peer-reviewed data deterministically with a full audit record.

Proc. Royal Society B CC BY 4.0
