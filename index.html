What ARIA is

ARIA is a deterministic behavioural intelligence substrate — it observes operational and behavioural signals, evaluates them against transparent, versioned rules, and produces a governed decision together with a verifiable record of how that decision was reached. The same inputs always produce the same outputs, and the reasoning behind every decision is inspectable after the fact.

What it does

Records, doesn't decide

ARIA is non-agentic. It captures and verifies evidence about governed decisions — it does not make those decisions itself.

Tamper-evident by construction

Records are cryptographically hashed and can be anchored externally, so any after-the-fact alteration is detectable, not merely disallowed by policy.

Flags drift, doesn't act on it

Part of that evaluation includes comparing current behaviour against an established baseline and surfacing meaningful deviation to a human reviewer — ARIA flags, it never acts automatically.

Built for OEM licensing

Designed as a substrate other products integrate into their own governed workflows — not a standalone end-user application.
