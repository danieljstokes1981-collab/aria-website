What ARIA Is

ARIA is a deterministic behavioural‑intelligence substrate for governed decisions. It observes operational and behavioural signals, evaluates them against transparent, versioned rules, and produces decisions with cryptographically verifiable audit records.

Core Principles

ARIA doesn’t replace your governance—it makes it observable, auditable, and tamper‑evident.

  • Records, doesn’t decide — ARIA captures and verifies evidence without making decisions itself.
  • Tamper‑evident by construction — Every record is hashed and chained so any alteration becomes visible.
  • Flags drift, doesn’t act — ARIA surfaces deviations but never auto‑corrects.
  • Built for OEM licensing — ARIA integrates as an embedded audit spine inside governed workflows.
