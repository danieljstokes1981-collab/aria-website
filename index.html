What it does

ARIA is designed to capture compliance-relevant evidence as part of the governed decision itself, rather than reconstructing it afterward.

It doesn't decide anything and it doesn't act. ARIA observes a governed decision as it happens and produces one thing: a record of it that can be checked, not just trusted.

Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof-claimed

Every record carries its own content hash and an explicit list of exactly which fields compose it. Alter anything afterward and the check fails -- that's demonstrated above, not asserted here.

Rules pinned to a version, not a memory

Each record states which edition of the regulatory text and indicator vocabulary produced it. If the underlying rules change later, older records stay distinguishable from newer ones.

A component, not a verdict

ARIA doesn't instruct and doesn't decide. Every record ends with the same line, because it's true every time: human review required.

How a record gets made

One decision, four stages, one record.

This is the actual sequence ARIA runs for every governed decision -- not a simplified diagram, the real pipeline.

01

Raw signal computed

Indicator scores are computed from the incoming metadata, before any suppression or gating is applied.

02

Suppression applied

Signals are bounded and floored to a safe, deterministic range before anything downstream sees them.

03

Regulatory gate applied

Context decides whether raw or suppressed scores are used -- for example, raw signal is surfaced when an inspection or enforcement action is already active.

04

Record generated

A GroundTruthRecord is produced: hashed, versioned, and bound back to the session it came from -- ready for human review.

The record itself

What's actually in it.

Not a marketing description of the record -- the real field names, the same ones an OEM licensee's engineers would see.

record_hashSHA-256 of the record's own content. Recomputed and checked on every read.
hashed_fieldsExplicit list of exactly which fields the hash covers.
regulation_versionWhich edition of the applicable rules produced this record.
taxonomy_versionWhich version of the indicator vocabulary this record's signals refer to.
source_referenceA pointer back to the originating session, for the licensee's own audit trail.
schema_versionWhich version of the record's own shape this is.
Applied example

One of the decisions ARIA is built to leave a trail for.

Safeguarding -- one of several verticals

A Designated Safeguarding Lead deciding whether to escalate a flagged concern.

Get it wrong in either direction and someone is affected -- a child left at risk, or a family or staff member harmed by a false escalation. That decision usually leaves no trail: what was seen, what triggered it, who decided, and why. ARIA's record captures exactly that, at the moment the decision is made -- not reconstructed afterward for a review.

This is one applied example, not the whole product. ARIA is built vertical-agnostic -- the same record structure applies wherever a governed decision needs to be shown, not just told.
